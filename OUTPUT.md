# SMS Spam Detection — Project Output & Empirical Evaluation

This document presents the consolidated real experimental results generated by executing `main.py` on the benchmark **UCI SMS Spam Collection** dataset.

---

## 1. Execution Summary

- **Dataset**: `data/SMSSpamCollection`
- **Original Messages**: 5,572
- **After Deduplication and Cleaning**: 5,166 messages (4,513 Ham, 653 Spam)
- **Train/Test Split**: 80:20 stratified holdout split (`random_state=42`)
  - **Training Set**: 4,132 messages (3,610 Ham, 522 Spam)
  - **Testing Set**: 1,034 messages (903 Ham, 131 Spam)
- **Feature Extraction**: TF-IDF (unigrams + bigrams, `min_df=2`, `max_features=3000`) fitted only on training data to prevent data leakage.

## 5. What-If Experiment: TF-IDF Vocabulary Capacity on KNN

*(Controlled experiment evaluating KNN with `max_features` = 500, 3000, 5000 under identical data split)*

| `max_features` | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1-Score |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **500** | 0.9700 | 0.9310 | 0.8244 | 0.8745 |
| **3000** | 0.9758 | 0.9818 | 0.8244 | 0.8963 |
| **5000** | **0.9787** | **0.9823** | **0.8473** | **0.9098** |

*Source: `results/what_if_tfidf_features.csv`*

![What-If Experiment](results/what_if_experiment.png)

**Key Finding**: Increasing `max_features` from 500 to 5,000 gives KNN's cosine distance metric sharper discriminative capability, boosting spam recall from 82.44% to 84.73% and elevating F1-score from 0.8745 to 0.9098.

---

## 6. Conclusion: Best-Performing Algorithm

1. **Overall Best Model**: **K-Nearest Neighbours (KNN)**
   - Achieves the highest spam **F1-score (0.8963)** and highest overall **Accuracy (0.9758)**.
   - The cosine distance metric effectively normalizes message length and measures angular orientation, yielding high precision (0.9818) with only 2 false positives out of 903 ham messages.
2. **Zero False-Alarm Filter**: **Multinomial Naïve Bayes**
   - Achieved a flawless **1.0000 Precision** (`FP = 0`), guaranteeing that zero legitimate personal messages are misclassified into spam.
3. **Decision Tree Limitation**:
   - Suffered from 18 false positives due to rigid single-word binary decision rules, resulting in the lowest precision (0.8594) and lowest F1-score (0.8494).
